## About Me

I'm **Kgothatso Theko**, usually called KG.

I have **5+ years of experience** building software across full-stack applications, systems integration, automation, Salesforce, and GTM systems.

I like understanding a problem well enough to build something impactful, then staying with it long enough to make it better.

My work has taken me from web and mobile applications into business systems, API integrations, CRM development, workflow automation, and AI-assisted development. I enjoy moving between technical layers and understanding how they fit together rather than treating them as separate problems.

I'm especially interested in the space between disciplines: where software meets business processes, where systems meet people, and where different ideas can be connected to solve a problem that initially looks unrelated.

**Kaizen** is the philosophy I return to: notice the friction, make a useful change, and learn from what happens.

## How I Approach Building

I want to understand the problem before deciding what to build.

I care about both sides of a system: the person using it and the person who will maintain it. That means paying attention to details that are easy to overlook — a qualification rule, a validation check, a clear error message, a useful commit, or an unnecessary step in a workflow.

I learn best by building, testing, breaking things, and improving them.

The common thread across my projects is curiosity: connecting ideas from different areas and turning those connections into something practical.
